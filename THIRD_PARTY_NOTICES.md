# Third-party materials

We depend on NumPy, SciPy, PyWavelets, Pillow, OpenCV, tqdm, PyTorch,
Diffusers, Matplotlib and invisible-watermark. We do not redistribute their
installed source trees. Their upstream licenses apply separately.

The native scripts refer to external Stable Diffusion XL and original
CompVis Stable Diffusion model/code assets. We do not redistribute those
assets, their weights, or the safety-replacement image.

We used COCO and DiffusionDB in the study. Dataset access and image reuse
follow the original providers' terms and the rights of the individual image
owners. We redistribute no prompt text and no dataset images other than the
thumbnails below.

The overview figure (`docs/assets/audit_flow.png`) reproduces manuscript
Fig. 1. It contains reduced thumbnails of COCO val2017 image
`000000555050.jpg`, its public-payload insertion and the derived difference
map 10|x'-x|, and of DiffusionDB 2M image
`ab135d33-6e5d-4393-8c52-ab01d096f238.png` (part-000001). These thumbnails
remain subject to the terms above.

The channel-survival plot contains our numerical visualization.
